How the menu is used, with the buttons your config has.
Controls
Point and press: Point at the menu with either hand, and pull the trigger on what the ray is on.
Scroll: Push the thumbstick up or down on the hand that points: the list the ray is on scrolls.
Step a value: Push the thumbstick left or right on the hand that points: the slider or the choice the ray is on steps.
Back: Tap the grip on either hand, pointing at the menu: one step back, and from the menu's front page the menu closes.
Move the menu: Long press the grip on either hand for 0.3 s, pointing at the menu: it follows the hand until you let go of the button.
Recenter: Long press the thumbstick on the off hand: the menu is put in front of you again, also after you moved it.
Open and close the menu: Long press the thumbstick on the primary hand for 1.5 s, holding the thumbstick on the off hand.
Minimize: Minimize, on the title bar or on your offhand wrist, hides the menu as you left it. Resume on the wrist shows it again.
Saving
Save: Writes the changes of the mod you are on, and the mod acts on them in its own way. A mod can say when: a changed row then has its mark, and the help line its tag.
Live: As you change it. Discard takes it back.
Save: After Save.
Restart: After the game is restarted.
No mark: The mod does not say, as no MCM mod does. Save writes it all the same.
